01

The moodboards

Iryna's own references. One Neptunian / Pisces vibe at two saturations: a vivid iridescent dream (boards 1–3) and a muted warm dream (board 4). Shared thread: light, water, aura, translucency.

02

What we studied

Reference is an input, not an output. Techniques pulled from real products — never cloning one — and a map of where the category goes wrong.

Benchmark

CHANI, torn down

Profiled from a real screen recording — warmer and more playful than its reputation: a three-voice type system and hand-drawn squiggle dividers.

The field

Competitors on Mobbin

Two traps: Co-Star's cold grayscale, and the purple-cosmic starfield (Nebula, Moonly). Closest to us: warm document (Tolan), photographic warmth (Calm).

The taste bar

Elevated references

mymind, Cosmos, Vyrao — dreamy done expensively. Type leads, one contained glow, matte not glossy, negative space.

03

Taste & attributes

Named references

  • mymind — restraint is the design
  • ABY Journal — legible text on a dreamy ground
  • CHANI — the type & voice benchmark

Anti-references (confirmed)

  • Aura, Opal, Air — cheap SaaS glassmorphism
  • Dark purple-cosmic starfield
  • Cold grayscale SaaS · the “cozy” cream+terracotta reflex

Six attribute pairs (each from data)

Expressive display serif ↔ neutral body← personas: reads like a real astrologer
Warm misted ground ↔ cool iridescent accent← report + Glow register
Editorial density (report) ↔ airy app spacing← the report is the paid deliverable
Iridescence in one object ↔ no illustration← honest about mysticism
Dream at full ↔ dream at low← wow-moment vs legible reading
Elevated restraint ↔ cheap glassmorphism← Iryna's taste (mymind in, Aura out)
