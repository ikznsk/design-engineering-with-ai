**Type modifier (not a standalone search term):** *expressive* — display serifs and glowing/chrome letterforms carry the whole mood in the boards. Use it inside a query (`expressive serif`), never alone.

## How the 10 split across the two registers

- **Register A — vivid iridescent dream** (wow-moment, chart reveal): 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8
- **Register B — muted warm dream** (report, checkout, long-form reading): 2, 4, 9, 10
- **Shared spine (present in both):** 2 translucent, 3 luminous, 4 blurred — these are the connective tissue. A reference that has *only* Register A words is a trend poster; a reference that has *only* Register B words is quiet-luxury wellness. **The ones worth collecting have at least one word from each column.**

## Stop-list — what to exclude from results

From `moodboard-analysis.md` ("what this rules OUT") plus the Impeccable calibration on saturated AI-default looks:

- **dark cosmic / starfield / neon purple** — the Nebula/Moonly category default
- **cold grayscale / clinical editorial** — Co–Star's register, not this taste
- **flat corporate SaaS** — no atmosphere, no texture
- **near-black + one neon accent + glowing edges** — a known AI-generated default; superficially matches "glow" but is the opposite of warm-luminous
- **cream ground + high-contrast serif + terracotta accent** — also a known AI-generated default. ⚠️ Register B sits dangerously close to this one. When collecting Register B references, demand a *material* (drapery, sand, smoke, stone, water) — not just cream paper and a serif. Cream + serif alone is the default wearing the moodboard's clothes.

## Reading the results (Impeccable rule)

A saved reference only counts if you can name its **system**, not its mood: palette, material, type stack, topology, controls and states, responsive behavior. "Nice glow" is not a reference. Screenshot the hero *and* one dense content screen from every site kept — Astro Recipe's hard problem is the paid report (long-form text under a dream), and hero-only references say nothing about it.
