Astro Recipe · Visual Concept · Lesson 6

A dream you can read.

An astrology product that turns a natal chart into concrete guidance — how to dress, position your career, run your content, manage your energy. This deck walks the look: from references, to taste, to three directions, to the chosen language — Liquid Aura.

01

The starting point — the moodboards

Iryna's own references. They read as one Neptunian / Pisces vibe at two saturations: a vivid iridescent dream (boards 1–3) and a muted warm dream (board 4). Shared thread: light, water, aura, translucency.

02

What we studied

Reference is an input, not an output. We pulled techniques from real products — never cloning one — and mapped where the category goes wrong.

Benchmark

CHANI, torn down

Profiled from a real screen recording — warmer and more playful than its reputation: a three-voice type system (serif + sans + mono) and hand-drawn squiggle dividers.

The field

Competitors on Mobbin

Two traps to avoid: Co-Star's cold grayscale, and the purple-cosmic starfield (Nebula, Moonly). Closest to us: warm document (Tolan) and photographic warmth (Calm).

The taste bar

Elevated references

mymind, Cosmos, Vyrao — dreamy done expensively. What separates it from cheap: type leads (not the gradient), one contained glow, matte not glossy, negative space.

03

Taste & attributes

Named references

  • mymind — restraint is the design
  • ABY Journal — legible text on a dreamy ground
  • CHANI — the type & voice benchmark

Anti-references (confirmed)

  • Aura, Opal, Air — cheap SaaS glassmorphism
  • Dark purple-cosmic starfield
  • Cold grayscale SaaS · the “cozy” cream+terracotta reflex

Six attribute pairs (each from data)

Expressive display serif ↔ neutral body← personas.md: reads like a real astrologer
Warm misted ground ↔ cool iridescent accent← report.md + Glow register
Editorial density (report) ↔ airy app spacing← the report is the paid deliverable
Iridescence in one object ↔ no illustration← honest about mysticism
Dream at full ↔ dream at low← wow-moment vs legible reading
Elevated restraint ↔ cheap glassmorphism← Iryna's taste (mymind in, Aura out)

Three directions

All luminous, all inside the moodboards — but different temperatures. Iryna chose Direction 02.

Direction 01

Moon Dream

Pastel iridescent cloud-sky + fashion serif. The dreamiest — kept for the wow-moment.

Chosen
Direction 02

Liquid Aura

Direction 03

Golden Hour

Warm sacred light + ring-of-light halo. Kept for the report's keepsake warmth.

05

The chosen language — Liquid Aura

One iridescent aura holds the chart; everything else stays calm and readable. The dream lives at the branding layer; the interface underneath stays functional.
