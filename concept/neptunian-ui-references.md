> **Why this file:** the moodboards (`Visual refferences /`, analyzed in `moodboard-analysis.md`) set the **branding** mood — Neptunian/Piscean: illusory, dreamy, translucent, iridescent, "between dream and waking." ## The core principle — how "dreamy" stays functional

All three searches converged on the **same four rules**. This is the reusable recipe:

1. **Atmosphere in the background layer; content stays opaque.** The gradient / glow / mist lives in the *ground*. Real text and controls ride on a **frosted-glass card, a white card, or a dark scrim** — almost never directly on a saturated gradient. The dream is the ground; the content gets its own clean plate.
2. **Glow goes behind objects, never behind text.** Aura/halo effects bloom behind a hero object (chart, moon, logo) or from empty screen-center. The moment real copy appears, the glow is capped by a scrim or moved out from under it.
3. **One solid CTA per screen.** However ethereal the field, the primary action is a single **opaque** pill (white or solid accent). It's the reliability anchor that lets everything else be soft.
4. **Warm/light grounds + dark text; white text only on deep bands.** The on-brief warm examples use near-black type on pale iridescent grounds. White text appears only where the gradient goes genuinely deep. This fork is exactly what keeps it out of "dark-cosmic" territory.

**Frosted panels are the workhorse for density.** Wherever there's real content — pricing tiers, forms, dashboards, reading blocks — it sits on a translucent-white or solid card. Backdrop-blur + a faint border/soft shadow keeps the glassy look while restoring contrast. Reserve full transparency for low-text decorative tiles.

## Honest availability note + what to avoid
- **The astrology category itself skews dark-cosmic** (Co–Star grayscale; Moonly/Moonlitt/Orbit deep-purple starfields). The warm-luminous style you want lives mostly in **wellness / meditation / journaling** apps — TIDE, ABY Journal, Ten Percent Happier, Blinkist, Lovi, Aura Health. Borrow from that neighborhood, not the astrology default.
- **True oil-on-water iridescence behind dense functional content is the rarest** — study ABY "ABY Noticed", Ten Percent Happier tiles, WeTransfer Core. Single-hue soft auras are far more common; compose the iridescent-over-content look yourself using the frosted-card technique.
- **Dashboards in this style are the scarcest** (Origin is the main usable example) — expect to compose that from the hero + frosted-card patterns.
- **Avoid:** CHANI's *marketing* site (distressed collage, not glass — though its in-app blush/lavender is on-brief); and the cold near-black "aurora" tools (Linear, Raycast, Arc) — that's the dark end the brief rules out.
