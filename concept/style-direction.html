The recipe

Four rules keep the dream functional

Every reference we studied — TIDE, ABY, Aura, Air — obeys the same discipline. This is how ethereal stays operable.

01

Atmosphere in the background

Gradient, glow and mist live in the ground layer. Text and controls ride on a frosted or solid card — never on the busy part of the gradient.

02

Glow behind objects, not text

The aura haloes the chart, the moon, the hero. The moment real copy appears, the glow is capped by a scrim or moved out from under it.

03

One solid CTA per screen

However soft the field, the primary action is a single opaque pill. It is the anchor that lets everything else be weightless.

04

Warm light grounds, dark text

Near-black type on pale iridescent grounds. White text only where a gradient goes genuinely deep — the fork that keeps us out of dark-cosmic cliché.
