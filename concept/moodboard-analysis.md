## Confirmed guiding vibe (Iryna, 2026-07-26)

> "Ілюзорний, розпливчастий, мрійливий, з легкими напівпрозорими текстурами — в астростилі це був би стиль **Риб**. Мрійливий, де сон, де яв, **нептуніанський**, десь із інших вимірів."

The spine of the whole visual identity is **Neptunian / Piscean**: dream-state, illusion, dissolving boundaries, the veil between worlds. In visual terms that means **translucency, soft blur / soft focus, iridescence (oil-on-water), mist and cloud, watercolor bleed, halos and aura, light diffusing through water.** Not hard-edged, not literal, not clinical — everything slightly out-of-focus, between dream and waking, "from another dimension."

This reframes the two boards below: they are **not opposing poles — they are the same Neptunian vibe at two saturations.** Boards 1–3 = the *vivid iridescent dream*; Board 4 = the *muted warm dream*. Same soul (translucency, diffusion, otherworldliness), different opacity. So the design question is not "which aesthetic wins" but **"how saturated is the dream on each surface."**

## The two registers of the one vibe

Both are warm and luminous — neither is cold SaaS, and (notably) **neither is the dark purple-cosmic cliché** the competitors default to. They are two dials on the same Neptunian dream.

### Register A — vivid iridescent dream (Boards "Copy", 2, 3)
The higher-saturation register. Literally the product's name — *Glow* — rendered as light. Neptunian in full colour.

- **What it looks like:** iridescent / holographic gradients, aura-glow halos, chrome-liquid 3D blobs, star-flare sparkles, dreamy pastel clouds, prism rainbows, shimmering water, glitter. Expressive glowing/chrome display type ("MOON DREAM", "Dreamland", "Galactic", "Every Day's a Good Day", "yet to come", "LOST WITHIN VOID").
- **Palette:** iridescent pastels — lavender, cotton-candy pink, aqua/cyan, peach, holographic rainbow; warm sunset oranges; teal water shimmer. High luminosity, everything glows from within.
- **Mood:** magical, feminine, aspirational, Instagram-native, spiritual-Y2K / "aura" aesthetic. Dopamine, wonder, "something enchanted is happening."

### Register B — muted warm dream / sacred minimal (Board 4)
The low-saturation register. Same Neptunian translucency (smoke, drapery, dandelion seed, robed figure in a ring of light — all "other-dimensional"), just quieter and warmer.

- **What it looks like:** warm sand / cream / taupe / ivory neutrals; soft directional natural light and long shadows; drapery, water ripples, rippled sand; classical arches and columns; ritual objects — incense smoke, dandelion seeds, a gold locket, iridescent shells, a robed figure inside a ring of light.
- **Palette:** monochrome warm neutrals — bone, oat, sand, greige, soft gold. Almost no saturated color; light itself is the accent.
- **Mood:** calm, meditative, expensive, timeless, editorial. "Clean-girl" quiet luxury meets sacred ritual. Understated, grown-up, trustworthy.

## Recurring motifs across all boards
Clouds · sparkle / star-flares · water shimmer & ripples · iridescence / holographic foil · soft 3D chrome blobs · sunset & golden light · celestial (moon, sun, stars) · smoke / incense · drapery & soft fabric · dandelion seeds · gold jewelry · arches. **Light and water are the connective tissue** — luminosity is the through-line that unites both poles.

## Type signals in the boards
Expressive **display** faces dominate: retro/fashion serifs (Astro Attic), glowing/ethereal serifs, chrome-liquid letterforms, airy wide-tracked caps. This confirms the **expressive-serif-display** direction from `references.md` #1 and the CHANI/Tolan evidence — but the *glowing/chrome* treatment is trend-coded and can date fast; the elegance of Board 4 implies a more restrained, timeless cut is the safer base.

## What this rules OUT (useful confirmations)
- **Not** Co–Star's cold grayscale editorial — her taste is warm and luminous, not austere/clinical.
- **Not** Nebula/Moonly dark purple-cosmic — even the "celestial" boards reach for *iridescent light*, not dark starfield + neon.
- **Not** flat corporate SaaS — everything has atmosphere, glow, texture.
This tightens the target: **warm + luminous + expressive**, avoiding all three category traps.

## How the dream is applied (the saturation gradient)

The whole product is Neptunian; the only variable is **how saturated the dream is on each surface** — and that's driven by one real constraint: **text has to stay readable, and the paid report has to feel like a $50 document, not a blurry poster.** So the dream turns *up* where the job is emotional and *down* where the job is reading.

- **Wow-moment / chart reveal — dream at full (Register A).** Iridescent ambient glow, oil-on-water gradient, soft halo behind the chart, mist. This is where "de son, de yav" lives and where the product earns its name. Exactly `references.md` #4 (Aurora / Ethereal Glow), used here at full strength.
- **Reading, report, checkout — dream at low (Register B).** Warm translucent ground (a soft misted off-white, *not* stark paper), restrained expressive serif, generous space, faint diffuse textures behind content rather than under it. Legible, premium, still dreamy — the fog is in the background, never over the words. Matches `references.md` #2 Glow, #3 astrologer report, Tolan/CHANI evidence.
- **Connective tissue everywhere:** translucency and soft blur as the default surface treatment (frosted layers, semi-transparent cards, halos on interactive elements), and **one iridescent accent** (blush / lavender / peach — oil-on-water shift) on CTA, selected tier, and the glow. This keeps even the quiet surfaces unmistakably part of the same dream.

Net: **one Neptunian dream, dialed up at the wow-moment and down under long-form text** — so it never dates into pure holographic trend and never sacrifices the report's legibility.

**Neptunian device kit** (concrete, buildable): frosted-glass / backdrop-blur layers · soft radial aura-glows · oil-on-water iridescent gradients · misted/foggy off-white grounds · watercolor-bleed edges · halos on the chart and CTAs · semi-transparent overlapping cards · very soft drop-shadows, no hard borders.

---

## The one open decision
Vibe and structure are set. The single remaining call is **how far down the dream dims under long-form text** — a legibility trade-off:

1. **Protect legibility hard** — under the report/readings the background goes near-solid warm off-white; translucency lives only in accents, dividers, and section openers. Safest for a paid document.
2. **Keep it dreamier throughout** — faint iridescent fog stays visible even behind body text (low opacity), accepting a little less contrast for more atmosphere everywhere.

My lean: **#1 for the paid report specifically** (it has to read like a keepsake), **#2 is fine for teaser/emotional screens.** But this is your call on taste-vs-readability.
